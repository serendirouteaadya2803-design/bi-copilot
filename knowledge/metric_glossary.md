# Metric Glossary (company knowledge base)

Sales: net revenue in rupees = units sold x list price x (1 - discount). Reported in Rs lakh (1 lakh = 100,000) or Rs crore (1 crore = 100 lakh).
Units: number of items sold.
Net price per unit: sales divided by units; the average price customers actually paid after discount.
Discount %: average promotional discount given on list price.
Profit: gross profit in rupees after cost of goods sold.
Profit margin: profit divided by sales, shown as a percentage.
Decline: a fall in sales when the current period is compared with the comparison period. By default the copilot compares the latest quarter (Jul-Sep 2026, fiscal Q2) with the previous quarter (Apr-Jun 2026).
QoQ: quarter on quarter, current quarter versus the previous quarter.
MoM: month on month, current month versus the previous month.
YoY: year on year, current period versus the same period one year earlier.
Volume effect: change in sales caused by selling more or fewer units, holding price constant.
Price effect: change in sales caused by a change in net price per unit (including discount changes), holding units constant.
Driver analysis: breaking the total change in sales into the contribution of each category, city or channel to find what caused the change.
Regions: North, South, East, West. South covers Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Kochi.
Channels: Online (website and app) and Retail Store.
